Why it sticks

Reading is not learning. Every course here is built around doing, testing, and remembering.

⚡ Interactive everything

Clickable architecture diagrams with animated step-through flows, and simulators you can break: trace a packet through a VPC, watch a quorum write, budget a context window.

🧠 Spaced repetition built in

Every module ships flashcards on a Leitner schedule (1/3/7/14 days). The sidebar shows what's due; ten minutes a day keeps it all retained.

🎯 Scenario-based testing

No trivia. Quizzes and timed exams pose realistic situations and dissect every option — including why the tempting wrong answers are wrong.

🛠️ Real-world missions

Project briefs shaped like actual work: greenfield builds, on-call incidents, migrations — done in your own accounts, with acceptance criteria and teardown checklists.

📝 Capture what you spot

Select anything and save it as a note. Your "still need to learn this" list lives in the course, syncs across devices, and links back to where you found it.

🔄 Live progress sync

Sign in with Google once per device and everything — lessons, scores, cards, notes, even where you left off — follows you in real time. Or stay signed out and it all just lives in your browser.

Free. Actually free.

No accounts required, no trials, no locked chapters, no "premium tier". These courses exist because learning the right things shouldn't have a toll booth.
