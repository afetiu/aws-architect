The course as a living cloud.

Six prototypes of what the course could become: fluid, game-like for adults, infographic-first, and built on simulations of the services themselves so you feel what S3 or a queue is before you read about it.

Touch before text One idea per screen Numbers you can drag Calm juice, no confetti 60 fps on a phone
01 · Living Board hero

An isometric VPC with real traffic

Requests flow as light across subnets, gateways and endpoints. Tap any block for what it is, scrub a request through its path step by step, flip the gateway-endpoint toggle and watch the same bytes take a different road while the NAT bill falls.

Replaces: click-to-highlight diagrams · Pattern: packets on paths, scroll/scrub as the explanation, failure and cost as things you see
02 · Service Sandbox

A mini S3 console you can break

Upload, delete, flip Block Public Access, drag time forward and watch objects sink through storage tiers while the bill drops. Every action logs what the API really did.

Replaces: slider simulators · Pattern: fake-AWS state machine, "under the hood" trace, break it on purpose
03 · Living Sim

Spam the button. Meet the queue.

You are the producer. Drop requests, add SQS, scale consumers, set the visibility timeout too short and watch duplicates appear, send poison messages to a dead-letter queue. One problem, one fix, seven chapters.

Replaces: the SQS lesson text · Pattern: Sam Rose–style escalating sim, draining-ring timers
04 · Constellation

Your progress as a star chart

Twenty-six modules as a pannable constellation. Mastery lights a node and draws lines to its neighbours; the frontier breathes; domain rings close only when you prove it again later.

Replaces: the module list and "lessons done" counter · Pattern: Khan-style mastery, Exercism frontier, rings that close
05 · Story

A lesson you scroll through

"How S3 keeps your data" as a pinned figure that changes as you scroll. Key terms in the sentences are chips: tap "3 AZs" and the picture re-lays out. Scroll is the scrubber.

Replaces: collapsible text sections · Pattern: Ciechanowski on a phone, scrubbable sentences, scroll-driven infographic
06 · Architect mode

Fill the gap in the architecture

A scenario, a diagram with one glowing empty slot, four service cards. Drag the right one in and the traffic starts flowing while the cost or availability meter moves. Wrong card floats back with a one-line "why not".

Replaces: multiple-choice scenario quizzes · Pattern: AWS Card Clash loop, consequence meters, the diagram is the grade

How they become one app

  1. Home = the Constellation. Not a dashboard of counters: a map that lights up. "Next for you" is one glowing node.
  2. Every module opens on its Living Board. The architecture of that module (VPC, S3 + CloudFront, SQS → Lambda…) with traffic running, before any text.
  3. Lessons are Stories. Pinned figure, scrollable beats, chips in the sentences. The existing plain-English layer (TL;DR, analogy, exam tip) becomes beats.
  4. Simulators become Sandboxes and Living Sims. The 16 slider widgets get rebuilt as state machines you can break, with an "under the hood" trace.
  5. Quizzes become Architect mode. Scenario cards you drag; consequence meters instead of "Correct!".
  6. Review stays spaced, but the cards are services you own, and mastery rings only close when you prove it again.

Prototypes, not the product. Vanilla JS, no build step, shared kit.css + kit.js. Design notes and research in the repo under docs/specs and docs/research. Sound is off until you turn it on in a prototype's top bar.
